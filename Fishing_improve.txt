Redesign and significantly enhance the entire Fishing Game to make it more immersive, exciting, and rewarding. The goal is to create a polished, high-engagement gameplay loop where every catch feels tense, unpredictable, and satisfying.

---

## Core Gameplay Improvements

* Completely overhaul the fishing and catching logic to remove repetitive and low-excitement outcomes.
* Redesign fish spawning and baiting systems so that fish variety feels dynamic, rare, and discovery-driven.
* Introduce meaningful rarity scaling:

  * Common fish should be easy to catch.
  * Rare and legendary fish should require skill, better equipment, and should feel genuinely difficult to hook and reel in.
* Add a risk-reward system where stronger fish are harder to control but give significantly better rewards.

---

## Fishing Mechanics Overhaul

* Implement an improved targeting system for rod casting and hook placement, giving players more control and skill expression.
* Rebuild line tension and reeling mechanics so that fish actively struggle and affect player control.
* Add a stamina/tension system for both fish and player interaction during catch sequences.

---

## Cinematic Camera & Animation

* Add dynamic cinematic camera angles when a fish is hooked:

  * Slow zoom-in on hook moment
  * Follow-cam while reeling in fish
  * Intense camera shakes during struggle moments
* Enhance all fishing animations:

  * Rod bending realism
  * Line tension physics
  * Fish thrashing, diving, and escaping behavior

---

## UI / UX Improvements

* Redesign the entire fishing UI to feel modern, immersive, and minimal during gameplay.
* Add a dedicated targeting interface for casting accuracy.
* Improve feedback systems (visual + audio) for hooks, catches, escapes, and rare discoveries.
* Make the experience feel like each catch is a “moment,” not a routine action.

---

## Fish Encyclopedia System

* Fully rebuild the Fish Encyclopedia:

  * Display all discovered and unlocked fish in a visually appealing catalog
  * Include fish models, rarity, habitat, and stats
  * Add progression tracking and completion rewards
* Encourage collection and discovery as a long-term gameplay goal.

---

## Equipment Expansion

Add deeper progression systems with new gear types:

### Rods

* Add 5 new fishing rods with unique stats and special abilities.

### Fishing Lines

* Introduce a new “Line” equipment category:

  * Example: Reinforced Nylon Line
  * Add 10 additional specialized lines with different durability, tension control, and rarity bonuses.

### Baits

* Add 5 new bait types that influence fish behavior, rarity chances, and biome-specific attraction.

---

## Progression & Reward System

* Add a meaningful progression loop where better gear unlocks access to more dangerous and rare fish.
* Introduce a reward system that increases excitement per catch:

  * Bonus loot for perfect catches
  * Rare “critical catch” moments
  * Combo or streak bonuses for successful catches

---

## Overall Design Goal

The game should feel:

* More thrilling with every cast
* Rewarding through discovery and rarity
* Skill-based rather than random
* Cinematic during key fishing moments
* Deep enough to support long-term progression and replayability

Every catch should feel like a moment of anticipation, tension, and payoff.
