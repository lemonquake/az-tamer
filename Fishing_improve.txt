# Fishing Game Complete Rework — Arcade Skill-Based Experience

## Vision

Completely remake the Fishing Game into a highly replayable arcade experience inspired by rhythm games and score attack systems. Every catch should feel intense, satisfying, and skill-based rather than passive or random.

The game should create a loop where players constantly say:

**"One more catch."**

---

# Core Philosophy

Fishing should no longer be a waiting simulator.

Fishing should become:

* Skill-based
* Combo-driven
* Challenging and rewarding
* Highly visual and exciting
* Addictive with score chasing
* Built around mastery rather than luck

Players should feel tension, excitement, and payoff from beginning to end.

---

# COMPLETE CATCHING SYSTEM OVERHAUL

## Rhythm-Based Reeling Mechanics

Remove simple hold-to-reel gameplay.

Replace it with active skill sequences inspired by rhythm games.

When a fish is hooked:

### Precision Inputs

Players must perform timed actions:

* Press E at the perfect moment.
* Alternate A and D when fish struggles.
* Hold and release Space during power pulls.
* React to random attack patterns from aggressive fish.

Different fish have different "fight patterns".

Examples:

### Salmon

Slow and forgiving.

Pattern:

E → Hold Space → E

### Tuna

Fast bursts.

Pattern:

A → D → E → Hold Space

### Shark

Chaotic and difficult.

Pattern:

E → A → D → E → Space → E

Legendary fish may have multiple phases.
