# 🎣 Comprehensive Fishing Expansion System

## Vision

Create a fully-featured, highly engaging Fishing Expansion that becomes a major side activity and progression path for players. Fishing should feel like its own game within the game, complete with progression, collectibles, exploration, economy systems, social interactions, rare discoveries, unique equipment, and meaningful gameplay rewards.

The system should be polished, visually impressive, fun for both casual and hardcore players, and designed for long-term replayability.

---

# 🌊 World Expansion

## The Great Pond

Construct a large scenic pond just outside the city walls.

### Location Design

* The pond should connect naturally to a river.
* The river flows southward beyond the city and disappears into the distant wilderness.
* Wooden docks extend into the water.
* Small fishing platforms are scattered around the shoreline.
* Tall grass, reeds, cattails, lily pads, and rocks decorate the area.
* Wildlife such as frogs, dragonflies, birds, turtles, and fish can be seen.
* Dynamic weather affects the area.
* Water reflects sunlight and moonlight beautifully.
* Day/night lighting should dramatically affect atmosphere.

### Interactive Areas

Players can:

* Fish from docks
* Fish from shoreline
* Fish from bridges
* Fish from special hidden locations
* Discover secret fishing spots

# 🎵 Dynamic Fishing Music System

Fishing mode must use a dedicated soundtrack system to enhance immersion and excitement.

### Background Music

* Upon entering Fishing Mode, immediately play:

  * `fishing.mp3`

This serves as the default ambient fishing soundtrack.

### Hooked Music

When a fish successfully bites and becomes hooked:

* Fade out `fishing.mp3`
* Play:

  * `fishing_hooked.mp3`

This track should create tension and excitement during the fish battle.

### Rare Fish Hook Music

If the hooked fish is:

* Rare
* Elite
* Mythical
* Legendary

Then immediately override the normal hooked music and play:

* `fishing_rare.mp3`

This alerts the player that they have encountered something valuable.

### Successful Catch Music

Upon successfully landing a fish:

* Play:

  * `fishing_capture.mp3`

This should accompany the fish showcase animation and catch summary.

### Legendary Capture Music

If the fish rarity is Legendary:

* Override all other catch music.
* Play:

  * `fishing_legendary.mp3`

This should feel grand, rewarding, and unforgettable.

### Music Transitions

* All music changes should smoothly fade between tracks.
* No abrupt audio cuts.
* Music should dynamically respond to fishing events.
