The Pitch

AZ Tamer is a lore-first, cinematic monster-taming RPG built as a custom WebGL experience. Players graduate the Tamer University of Aurel, pledge to a Grand House, and step into a world where the seal on a dimension of corrupted gods is thinning -- and the legends who sealed it have gone silent.

It is a love letter to classic creature-collecting, wrapped in a political thriller about erased records, buried empires, and a news network that writes tomorrow before it happens.

03Befriend, Never Break

Gift berries, win trust, and build a party of true bonds.

04Climb the Circuit

Rank up through sanctioned tournaments across four continents.

Strike to build SP, Guard to breathe, then cash in with Techniques. Ten elements multiply across every defender typing.

Customize hull, engine, legs, cannon, scanner, and cargo. Upgrade with Mystic parts once wielded by legends.

Multi-stage evolution lines, Aether studies, and fusion Guardians. Your team is a living pedigree.

Act II -- The Anomalies

Somebody Started Praying at the Door

A terror-cult worships the nine corrupted Guardians of Ghandra as imprisoned gods. They call the Legion War "the Great Theft" and the seal "the Ninefold Insult."

They surfaced quietly -- defaced shrines, stolen relics, missing wardens -- until the night they stopped being quiet: the invasion of Hyujon, the Lantern of the North.

Their four Factions each worship a "choir" of the nine. The deadliest -- the Crownless Procession -- is led by the Veiled Cantor, whose voice sounds like a struck bell.

FDChoir of the False Dawn

Zealot-priests of counterfeit sunrise. Burn the unworthy, convert the rest.

SWThe Stillwater Communion

Drowned-quiet infiltrators who call victims "the kept." Took Hyujon's harbor in one tide.

RTThe Rootless

Saboteurs of soil and stone who starve cities into prayer.

CPThe Crownless Procession

The head of the snake. Their project: walk the Hollow Crown out of Ghandra.

Act III -- The Turmal Run

An Island That Never Agreed to Land

Turmal floats above the western sea, held aloft by no map and no explanation. Its Seasonal Tournament is the loudest open circuit outside the Grand Coliseum -- and its Sponsors' gold is three layers laundered.

Players enter not for glory, but for access. Behind the hospitality boxes waits a freight elevator not on any blueprint, and at the bottom: a Rip in space and time.

"They took the wrong sister. Coming, Rin."
-- Azrael's last note, pinned where Onnel's chair could see it

Acts IV & V -- Built & Designing

The Rip & Foretales

The Rip

A 300-year-old wound beneath Turmal, fed by caged Guardian sacrifices. The Sponsors and the Anomalies were never partners -- they were always the same thing.

The Old Empire did not drown in ambition. It was bled. The Reliquary Ledger proves it.

Foretales

The four-continent news network doesn't report the world -- it drafts it. Ivan Lawrence was not their first deletion; he was a rehearsal.
