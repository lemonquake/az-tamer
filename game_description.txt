================================================================================
                                   AZ TAMER
                    A Monster-Taming Dungeon RPG & Battler
================================================================================

OVERVIEW:
---------
AZ Tamer is a rich monster-taming RPG set in the Age of Embers, a world where 
humans and elemental entities called Guardians form deep, mutual bonds. Players 
explore various towns and dungeons, build their dream team of Guardians, fish in 
deep lagoons, and climb the ranks of the prestigious Worldring Circuit to 
become the World Champion.

WORLD & LORE:
-------------
* The First Dawn (~3,000 Years Ago): 
  The Veil between the physical world and Ghandra thinned, allowing Guardians—
  beings of living elemental energy—to cross into the world.
  
* The First Bond (~700 Years Ago): 
  On Agdao Island, a fisher-girl named Aurelia saved a storm-wounded Guardian, 
  marking the first true bond based on trust rather than force.
  
* The Guild Compact (~400 Years Ago): 
  Tamers formed Guilds and signed the Compact to protect Guardians from chains, 
  ensuring all bonds are born of mutual trust.
  
* The Legion War (~15 Years Ago): 
  Three legendary Tamers (Aljay, Greggy, and Onnel) sealed the Corrupted Legion 
  back in the folded dimension of Ghandra. Today, that seal is weakening...

THE FOUR CONTINENTS:
--------------------
* Olivar: The Capital region, home to Haven City and the Leodones University.
* Veyra: The western sea-continent of a thousand harbors and mist-shrouded ruins.
* Tharkand: The eastern desert of red dunes and humming, ancient war-engines.
* Noruun: The frozen south, where ancient Ice Guardians sleep under glaciers.

CORE GAMEPLAY MECHANICS:
------------------------
1. Guardian Team Building:
   Collect and bond with a variety of Guardians across different elements 
   (Tide, Blaze, Verdant, Volt, Umbra, Gale) and evolution stages (Adept, 
   Elite, Apex, Legendary, Aether).

2. Tactical Turn-Based Combat:
   Engage in challenging battles where elemental typing, party synergy, and 
   move sets dictate victory. Sanctioned circuit matches award no EXP, meaning 
   your placement relies entirely on the strength and synergy of the team you built.

3. The Worldring Circuit:
   Register and participate in structured tournament tiers starting from Haven 
   City's Weekly Opens, climbing through the Turmal Seasonals, Continental Crowns, 
   the Playoffs, and ultimately the World Championship at Terra City's Worldring.
   Rank up your Universal Rank based purely on Tournament Points earned in the Ring.

4. Deep Fishing Minigame:
   Utilize different fishing rods and lures to catch a vast selection of fish species 
   in locations like Agdao Island's turquoise lagoons and New Salmonan's rivers.

5. Customization:
   Unlock achievements, change clothes, select custom portrait designs, and explore 
   distinct locations like the tech-ruins of Hyujon and the peaceful valleys of 
   New Salmonan.
